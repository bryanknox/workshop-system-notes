# Stompbox submodule

Physical Module: VCO

https://www.musicthing.co.uk/Workshop_Quick_Start/#stompbox

Stompbox is a send-and-return loop for guitar pedals.

A pedal power output is under the panel.
The output is 9V, centre negative, on a 2.1mm connector.
It supplies up to 300mA.

The Feedback knob is an attenuverter.
At 12 o'clock, the feedback is off.
At 5 o'clock and at 7 o'clock, the feedback is at maximum.
