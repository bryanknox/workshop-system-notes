# Stompbox submodule

Physical Module: VCO

https://www.musicthing.co.uk/Workshop_Quick_Start/#stompbox

It's a send-and-return loop for guitar pedals.

There's a 2.1mm 9V centre-negative pedal power output under the panel, rated up to 300mA, and the Feedback knob is an attenuator. At 12 o'clock it is off.
